Printer no printer

The first print opens the serial port chooser — pick the “Standard Serial over Bluetooth link” entry for the printer. The grant survives reloads on its own, so F5 does not send you back to the chooser and no flags are needed. Use “Forget saved printer” to revoke it and pick another.

A Bluetooth Classic (SPP) printer is invisible to Web Bluetooth and must go through this serial path. “Use a BLE printer instead” is only for printers that expose a GATT service — and reconnecting to one after a reload does need a Chrome flag.
